YOUR DECKS

Your talks and your brand stay yours

keystone/                the tool
  decks/                 yours: a git repository of its own
    keystone.json        your decks, your language
    my-talk/
      deck.json          title, language, style, order
      slides/cover/
        brief.json       what the slide must say
        slide.html       the slide
        slide.fr.json    its text in French
    public/              your logos, colours, site
  • Keystone comes with a neutral brand
  • A file in decks/public/ replaces the tool's own
  • Your talks never enter the tool's repository

A git pull updates the tool, never your slides.
