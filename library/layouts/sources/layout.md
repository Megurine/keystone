# Layout: sources

References after the talk, grouped by chapter in two columns; each one links to the source.

- Markup: `div.sa-sources` > two `div` columns > `section`s: `h3` (`<b>01</b>` chapter number + name) + `ul` of
  `li > a` (`strong` title, optional `em` precision; `span` author · venue; `code` short URL).
- Rail: SOURCES. Title: "Pour aller plus loin".
- A figure's own source goes on its slide, in `.sa-source` (see `components/caption`).
