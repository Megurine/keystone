# Layout: chapter

A numbered chapter opener. Same ground as the cover (red, photo, monogram, wordmark), with a two-digit number above a
bigger chapter title.

- Use: the first slide of each chapter. The deck's `CLAUDE.md` lists the chapters.
- Number: two digits (`01`, `02`), in `.sa-section-num`.
- Title: the chapter name in capitals (`sa-cover-title sa-section-title`). The rail of the chapter's slides shows the
  same name, or a short form of it (see `styles/rail`).
- Subtitle: optional `.sa-section-sub`, one short line (what the chapter covers, e.g. "UV space").
- Don't: add a rail or content. Un-numbered interstitials (demo, closing) have their own layouts.
