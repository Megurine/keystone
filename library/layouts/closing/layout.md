# Layout: closing

The last slide: cover ground, THANK YOU! high on the slide, then either a recap of what to remember or a QR code.

- Title: `h1.sa-cover-title.sa-section-title.is-high` (top 176).
- Recap: `div.sa-closing` > `p` (Takeaways) + `ul` of 3–5 short lines (one per chapter).
- Or a QR code: `div.sa-qr > img` (210×210 on white), then `p.sa-section-sub` (the theme moves it under the code) with
  a `<small>` second line.
- A sources slide may follow it (layout `sources`).
