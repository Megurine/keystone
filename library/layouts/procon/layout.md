# Layout: procon

Pros and cons of one technique in two columns, each under a heading with a +/− box. The usual end of a part
("Topic: the verdict"; in French "Sujet&nbsp;: le bilan").

- Markup: `div.sa-procon` > two `section`s, each `h3` (`<b>+</b> Advantages` / `<b>−</b> Drawbacks`) + `ul.sa-list`
  with `li.pro` / `li.con`. Top 160; under a lead, move it down on the slide (top 262).
- 2–5 items per column, one or two lines each. A figure or a takeaway may sit under the columns (from y ≈ 420).
- Don't: mix plain items in, more than two columns.
