# Component: takeaway

`p.sa-takeaway`: the closing line, at the bottom of the slide (56px from the bottom, 73px side margins) under a 1px
rule, Poppins 28. Same place on every slide.

- One sentence the audience must remember: a statement, or the question the next slide answers. The key phrase in
  `<span>` (red). No fixed prefix.
- It appears last: give it the largest `data-in` of the slide.
- Keep the content above y ≈ 600 when a takeaway is present.
